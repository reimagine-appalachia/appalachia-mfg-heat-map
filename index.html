Location quotient

What is a location quotient?

A location quotient (LQ) shows how concentrated an industry is in a county compared with the United States as a whole.

How it's calculated
LQ = county jobs in the industryall county jobs ÷ U.S. jobs in the industryall U.S. jobs

How to read it. An LQ of 1 means the industry makes up the same share of local jobs as it does nationally. Above 1, the county is more specialized than the nation; an LQ of 3 means the industry's share of local jobs is three times the national share. Below 1, it is less concentrated than average.

    In small counties a single plant can produce a very high LQ, so check the job count in the hover card alongside it.
